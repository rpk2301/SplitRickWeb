2. What SplitRick is (and isn't)

SplitRick is a mobile application that helps you and the people you share expenses with keep a shared record of who paid for what and what each person owes. It tracks transactions you and your group members enter, computes balances, and lets you record payments to settle those balances.

SplitRick is not a payment processor. We do not move money. We do not connect to your bank, credit card, or any other financial account. When you "record a payment" in SplitRick, you are telling SplitRick that a payment happened outside the app so the balances can be updated. You are responsible for any actual money movement, including any disputes about whether a payment was made or received.

Balances and amounts shown in SplitRick are informational records based on the entries you and your group members make. They are not statements of debt, legal obligations, or accounting records, and SplitRick does not verify the underlying transactions.

  • Invitations. A group invite link gives anyone who has the link the ability to join that group. Treat invite links as you would any other shared secret — share them only with people you actually want in the group. Group owners can regenerate or revoke invite codes.
  • Accuracy of entries. You are responsible for the accuracy of the transactions and payments you record. Editing or deleting a transaction notifies and re-balances the group; do not use this functionality to manipulate balances in bad faith.
  • Visibility of historical activity. If you leave a group, your historical contributions (transactions you added, balances you participated in) remain visible to the remaining members so the books stay consistent. Future members of a group can see prior activity in that group.
