Email support

We typically reply within 1–2 business days.

What we can help with

  • Trouble signing in. SplitRick signs you in with a one-time passcode (OTP) texted to your phone number. If a code isn't arriving, make sure you have signal and didn't reply STOP to our messages; then email us and we'll help.
  • Bugs & feedback. Tell us what happened and what you expected. Screenshots and your phone model / iOS version help us reproduce it faster.
  • Groups, balances & payments. Questions about how splitting, balances, or settling up work — we're happy to walk you through it.
  • Receipt scanning & other Pro features. Questions about SplitRick Divide and what it unlocks.

Manage or cancel SplitRick Divide

SplitRick Divide is an auto-renewable subscription billed through the Apple App Store. To manage, change, or cancel it, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone and select SplitRick. Apple handles all billing — see our Terms for details. If you have a billing question we can't resolve, email us and we'll point you in the right direction.

Delete your account

You can request deletion of your SplitRick account at any time. Email SplitRickUS@gmail.com from the phone number / address tied to your account, or use any in-app deletion control we provide. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what happens to your data when you delete.

Privacy & your data

For privacy questions, data-access requests, or anything about how we handle your information, email us or read the full Privacy Policy.
