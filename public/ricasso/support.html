Ricasso Support

Stuck, found a bug, or want to report something? The answers to the usual questions are below. If yours isn't here, send us a note.

Email SplitRickUS@gmail.com We usually reply within a couple of days.

Playing

How does a game work?

Each turn, one player picks a word and draws it while everyone else types guesses. Guess faster to score more; the drawer scores when people get it. Everyone takes a turn drawing each round, and the most points at the end wins.

How do I invite friends?

Tap Play to start a room, then tap the Join code in the top corner to share an invite link. Friends who tap it go straight to your lobby. They can also tap Join game and type the 5-letter code.

How many people can play?

2 to 24 players. Rooms with more than 12 players play up to 2 rounds, so the game doesn't run all night.

How does team mode work?

The host turns on Team mode in the lobby. Your teammates guess first; at half time the other team can steal. Each team needs at least 2 players to start, and you can switch teams in the lobby as long as the teams stay even.

What's the word of the day?

A new word every day at 6 AM (US Eastern). Tap the note on the home screen to sketch it on your own, whenever you like.

Your stuff

How do I change my name or avatar?

Tap your name in the top corner of the home screen. You can shuffle a new face, build one in Customize, or draw your own.

How do I save a drawing?

At the end of a game, tap any drawing to save or share it, or tap Save all. Ricasso asks for permission to add pictures to Photos; it never looks at your library.

I'm not getting turn alerts

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, then Ricasso, and turn on Allow Notifications. Alerts only arrive while you're in a game and have switched to another app.

I lost my connection mid-game

Ricasso reconnects on its own when your signal comes back. If you've been gone more than about a minute, the game moves on without you, but you can jump back in as long as the game is still going.

Safety

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the player in the lobby, or open the game menu (the round badge) and choose Report or block a player.

  • Report sends it to us to look at, with the room and turn so we can find the drawing.
  • Block hides their drawings, guesses and avatar from you in every game. Unblock them any time in Settings, Blocked players.
How do I remove someone from my room?

If you're the host, tap the player in the lobby, or use the game menu during a game, and choose Kick. They can't come back to that room.

How do I get my data removed?

Email us with your display name and roughly when you played, and we'll remove finished games and leaderboard entries with your name. See the privacy policy for what Ricasso keeps.
