Ricasso Privacy Policy

Last updated November 17, 2026

Ricasso is a drawing and guessing game. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we don't sell your data or use it for advertising. This page explains what the app sends to our servers, who can see it, and how long it's kept.

What other players see

Every Ricasso room is public: anyone can find it in the room list or join with its code or invite link. People in your room see:

  • your display name and avatar,
  • what you draw, live, as you draw it,
  • your guesses and your score.

When a game finishes, it's added to Recent games, where anyone using the app can see the players' names, avatars, scores and the drawings from that game. Your name, avatar and points also appear on the monthly leaderboard.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Display name and avatarTo show you to other players, in Recent games and on the leaderboard.
Drawings, guesses and scoresTo run the game, and to show finished games in Recent games.
A random ID for your copy of the appTo reconnect you to your seat, keep your leaderboard points together, and count how many devices use Ricasso. It isn't linked to your name, Apple ID or phone number.
App version, iPhone model and iOS version, each time the app opensTo know which versions are in use, make sure updates reach people, and fix device-specific problems.
Push notification token (only if you allow notifications)To tell you when it's your turn to draw after you've stepped away.
Words you suggestSo we can review them and add good ones to the game.

Ricasso doesn't collect your location, contacts, photos, email address or phone number. If you save a drawing, it goes to your Photos library on your phone and isn't uploaded.

How long it's kept

  • Rooms and everything in them (the live game, chat and connection details) are deleted automatically about a day after they're last used.
  • Finished games in Recent games, with their drawings, are kept so they can keep being viewed.
  • Leaderboards reset at the start of every month and are deleted about two months later.
  • App-open records (version, model and iOS version) are kept for 30 days; daily totals are kept for about a year.
  • Word suggestions are kept until reviewed; turned-down ones are deleted after 90 days.

Where it's stored

Ricasso runs on Amazon Web Services in the United States. Push notifications are delivered through Apple's push notification service.

Removing your data

You can change your name and avatar at any time in the app. To have finished games or leaderboard entries with your name removed, email us at SplitRickUS@gmail.com with your display name and roughly when you played, and we'll take care of it.

Children

Ricasso isn't directed at children under 13. Because rooms are public, players shouldn't use their real full name as their display name.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
